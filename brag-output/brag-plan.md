# Brag Plan: VitalsDeck (v2 Update)

## What is this app?
VitalsDeck is an ultra-lightweight, cyberpunk-inspired macOS menu-bar and floating HUD utility providing real-time hardware telemetry (Apple Silicon CPU/GPU temperatures, load meters), Caffeine sleep prevention mode, process termination, full modular settings, and 5 retro-futuristic color themes with near-zero CPU footprint.

## The angle
Activity Monitor is boring and takes half your screen. VitalsDeck lives right in your menu bar (showing live 46°C CPU/GPU temp) and opens into a full retro-futuristic flight deck with 5 distinct themes, instant sleep prevention (Caffeine), and process termination.

Cyberpunk glitch typography:
"ACTIVITY MONITOR IS BORING." → "MEET VITALS DECK."
"CYBERPUNK COCKPIT FOR APPLE SILICON"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Menu Bar & Live Telemetry (3.0s – 7.0s)**:
  - Real macOS Menu Bar snippet showing `46°C` with the pulse icon.
  - Signal Amber deck with real CPU & GPU temps, RAM breakdown, and ASCII meters.
  - Native libproc C syscalls: 0.6% CPU usage, zero battery waste.
- **5 Aesthetic Visual Themes (7.0s – 13.0s)**:
  - Matrix Green (CRT phosphor green)
  - Tokyo Neon (Cyber synthwave dual neon cyan/magenta)
  - OLED Mono (Stealth monochrome pure black)
  - Nord Frost (Arctic slate and polar teal)
- **Power Features & Modular Settings (13.0s – 18.0s)**:
  - **Caffeine Mode**: Keep Mac awake indefinitely or with timer (20m, 1h, 3h, ∞) with 1 click.
  - **Process Terminator**: Instant kill switch for background CPU/RAM hogs.
  - **Full Modular Settings**: English & Русский language toggle, customizable menu bar metrics, update intervals (Fast 1s, Balanced 2s, Eco 4s), and launch at startup.
  - Real Settings Window screenshot in 3D perspective.
