1. Real Apple Silicon hardware temperatures (46°C / 49°C) in Menu Bar & Popover.
2. Live ASCII load meters for CPU, GPU, RAM, Network.
3. 5 Cyberpunk aesthetic visual themes.
4. Caffeine Mode (sleep prevention with 1-click timer).
5. Process Terminator (instant 1-click KILL buttons).
6. Modular Settings (English / Русский, update interval, launch at startup).
